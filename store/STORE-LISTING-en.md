# Medical Simulator — Vale Edition

Build your career in a living hospital. Investigate symptoms, examine patients, request tests, make decisions under pressure, and face the consequences of every choice.

## Highlights

- 43 configured experiences across variable consultations, emergencies, and follow-ups;
- four difficulty profiles, from newcomer to professional challenge;
- career seasons, mentors, reputation, competencies, and specialization paths;
- patients with human context, dynamic trust, and stress;
- clinical academy, medical record, physiological evolution, and debriefing;
- Portuguese, English, and Spanish;
- offline play and accessibility features.

Fictional educational simulation. Not a medical device and not a replacement for training, care, or real-world protocols.
